Haibo Liu | 刘海波

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Computer Science, the University of Texas at Austin, hosted by Qixing Huang. Prior to UT, I obtained my Ph.D. degree from the Department of Mathematical Sciences at Tsinghua University, mentored by Junqing Chen, where I was honored to receive the Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation of Tsinghua University. I worked as a research assistant at Westlake University, working in Xiangru Huang's lab. Previously, I obtained my Bachelor's degree with honors in Mathematics at Lanzhou University.

My current research focuses on 3D Computer Vision.
