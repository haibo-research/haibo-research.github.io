I am honored to have received the Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation of Tsinghua University (PDF). I obtained my Bachelor's degree with honors in Mathematics at Lanzhou University.

My current research interests lie in the fields of Graphics, 3D Computer Vision, and Inverse Problems.
